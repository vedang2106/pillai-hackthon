# EventFlow AI: Crowd Dynamics & Safety Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## 1. Physical Crowd Density Physics & Safety Thresholds
In high-occupancy event venues, pedestrian flow and safety are governed by critical density thresholds measured in people per square meter ($\text{p/m}^2$):

- **Normal Flow ($< 1.5\text{ p/m}^2$)**: Pedestrians walk freely without speed reduction or physical contact.
- **Controlled Flow ($1.5\text{ to } 2.5\text{ p/m}^2$)**: Walking speed slows; minor directional adjustments needed.
- **Dense Flow ($2.5\text{ to } 3.5\text{ p/m}^2$)**: Physical contact between attendees becomes frequent; free movement is restricted.
- **Critical Threshold ($3.5\text{ to } 4.5\text{ p/m}^2$)**: Shockwaves can travel through the crowd. Severe risk of crowd turbulence and fallen attendees.
- **Extreme Danger ($> 5.0\text{ p/m}^2$)**: High risk of compressive asphyxiation and crowd crush. Immediate emergency dispersal protocol required.

## 2. Bottleneck Prevention & Flow Control Protocol

### Gate Throttle Mechanism:
When any entry gate experiences an inflow rate exceeding $40\text{ persons/minute/meter}$ of clear width:
1. Automatically activate outer queue holding bays to space out incoming arrivals.
2. Divert 40% of incoming queue traffic to secondary entry gates with $< 50\%$ utilization.
3. Deploy crowd marshals equipped with megaphone directionals to manage queue splitting.

## 4. Emergency Evacuation Directives

- **Phase 1 (Localized Clearance)**: Direct security to clear a 5-meter radius around any reported trip hazard or fallen attendee within 60 seconds.
- **Phase 2 (Zonal Evacuation)**: Open all auxiliary exit gates. Broadcast localized audio alerts in 2 languages advising attendees of designated exit routes.
- **Phase 3 (Full Venue Dispersal)**: Disable all incoming turnstiles. Set all automated barriers to permanent open state. Synchronize local traffic lights to green for egress avenues.
